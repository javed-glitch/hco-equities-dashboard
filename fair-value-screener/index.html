How to read this

Attractive means the price is at or below the cheap line: the P/E's average minus one spread over up to five years. Very attractive means two spreads under the average. Near the line means Fair value but within 8% above the cheap line. The spread is a robust one (median-based), as inferred from Stock Simplifier's charts.

Timing is the quant server's cycle stage for the stock, from daily RSI(14), the drop from the 52-week high and the 200-day average. ADD is Panic, Disbelief or Denial. DOUBLE DOWN is Anger, Capitulation or Depression. HOLD and TRIM mean no buy zone. Bounce confirmed means the price crossed back above its 5-day average after RSI was below 35 in the last 10 sessions. The rules are a line-for-line port of server.js and gave identical stage, RSI and confirmation on 14 test symbols.

Limit ladder is the same three-rung rule as /ladderdd without the options put wall (that comes from your Mac): rung 1 is the lower of 2% under the price and 1.5% under the 10-day low, rung 2 is 2.5% under rung 1, rung 3 is 3.5% under rung 2, split 40 / 40 / 20. Amounts are yours to set. For the put-wall version run /ladderdd in Telegram.

Price vs cheap line is negative when the price is below the line (the further below, the cheaper). Return to target is the 12-month analyst target against today's price.

Flagged rows are the company types where this method is weakest: zero or negative earnings (no P/E at all), under three years of history, two years of falling revenue. They are hidden by default, as are ranges that are too jumpy to trust. A range is also marked unreliable when the multiple's history has a heavy tail (ordinary spread above 60% of the average): Uber and AppLovin looked cheap on the robust spread but Stock Simplifier rated them Fair. Loss-makers cannot be scanned because only P/E is used.

How accurate is it? Yahoo only holds about 3 to 5 years of earnings, and its quarterly earnings mix split-adjusted and unadjusted values (corrected here). Against Stock Simplifier on 17 P/E symbols the zone matched on 13 (76%), the average was within 15% on 11 of 15, and today's multiple within about 2%. Spin-offs and one-off gains distort some averages. This is a first filter, not a buy list: check any name in the Fair Value Desk (Trading View data, plus Nash and Graham) before acting.

The universe is the ISUS holdings of 26 Oct 2026 that are also in the S&P 500, with Financials and Real Estate removed (119 symbols). Rebuild it when ISUS or the index changes.
