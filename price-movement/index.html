How the signal works - Stock
What it measures: the stock's price move over the last 1 month (30 days). What the % means: a percentage of the shares you already hold in that stock. It is not a percentage of your whole portfolio. Example: TRIM 30% means sell 30% of the shares you hold of that stock; BUY 15% means add shares equal to 15% of what you hold. If you hold none, there is nothing to size from, so use it as a prompt to review. The ladder is a guide, not advice.

How the signal works - ETF
Applies to: the core ETFs (VUSA, ISDU, ISUS, ISWD). What it measures: the ETF's price move over the last 6 months, not 1 month. ETFs move about a quarter as much as single stocks and drift upward, so the steps are smaller, the window is longer, and the levels are set so each step is about as rare as the stock one. What the % means: same as for stocks, a percentage of the units you already hold. Trims are half the size of the stock ladder and are a maximum, not an order: size them as you see fit. There are three trim rungs and no top rung. Trims only apply while core is above 50% of your account (check /interimledger; this page cannot see your holdings). Buys have no such condition. What history says (2009 to 2026): this ladder roughly matches simply holding, with slightly smaller drawdowns. Its value is deploying spare cash with discipline and a sensible rebalancing prompt, not beating buy-and-hold. A guide, not a forecast or advice.
