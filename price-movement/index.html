How the signal works - Stock
What it measures: the stock's price move over the last 1 month (30 days). What the % means: a percentage of the shares you already hold in that stock. It is not a percentage of your whole portfolio. Example: TRIM 30% means sell 30% of the shares you hold of that stock; BUY 15% means add shares equal to 15% of what you hold. If you hold none, there is nothing to size from, so use it as a prompt to review. The ladder is a guide, not advice.

How the signal works - ETF
Applies to: the core ETFs (VUSA, ISUS, ISWD). What it measures: the ETF's price move over the last 6 months, not 1 month. ETFs move about a quarter as much as single stocks and drift upward, so the steps are smaller, the window is longer, and the levels are set so each step is about as rare as the stock one. What the % means: same as for stocks, a percentage of the units you already hold. Trims are half the size of the stock ladder and are a maximum, not an order: size them as you see fit. There are three trim rungs and no top rung. Trims only apply while core is above 50% of your account (check /interimledger; this page cannot see your holdings). Buys have no such condition. What history says (2009 to 2026): this ladder roughly matches simply holding, with slightly smaller drawdowns. Its value is deploying spare cash with discipline and a sensible rebalancing prompt, not beating buy-and-hold. A guide, not a forecast or advice.

How to read this page
Prices from Yahoo Finance, as of each scan. WAIT / RECLAIMED / ABOVE (on buy and ADD rows) reads the 4H 5-bar SMA, with 4H bars built from Yahoo hourly data: WAIT = the last 4H bar is below its SMA, so deploy on a 4H close above the price shown; RECLAIMED = the latest 4H bar is the first close above it; ABOVE = above for several bars, so late. It is not the daily 5-day gate quant uses, and your chart is the reference. FELL / STOPPED (Cycle column, on the same rows): FELL = daily RSI was under 35 (ETFs 40) at some point in the last 10 sessions, so there was a real fall first (quant’s oversold gate); STOPPED = the 4H bar has reclaimed its 5-bar SMA (the same test as the chip); CONFIRMED = both. STOPPED is an earlier, looser bounce read than quant’s daily-close confirmation. The Cycle stage itself comes from the market-scan bot, which reads RSI on 4H bars, so its stage can differ from quant’s daily-RSI stage. Targets are analyst consensus. Cycle is the market-scan cycle stage, shown as published; a dash means that symbol isn't in the cycle scan yet. Symbols come from the shared hco-symbols list; “Gap” means the symbol is in the registry but the Options Gravity scan doesn't cover it yet. 30% Entry is the price at which a +30% gain would reach the 52-week high (or the analyst target). Highlighted price/entry cells mean the price is already below that entry. Upside to Tgt is the analyst target over the current price; Per £1,000 is that upside applied to £1,000 (a % figure, so currency-neutral). Satellite, swing and core stocks also follow their own rules elsewhere; this table is the simple monthly-move view. Data is for research, not advice.
