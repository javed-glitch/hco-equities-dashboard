How the signal works

What it measures: the stock's price move over the last 1 month (30 days). What the % means: a percentage of the shares you already hold in that stock. It is not a percentage of your whole portfolio. Example: TRIM 30% means sell 30% of the shares you hold of that stock; BUY 15% means add shares equal to 15% of what you hold. If you hold none, there is nothing to size from, so use it as a prompt to review. The ladder is a guide, not advice.
